MODERN RESIDENCE 3D ARCHITECTURAL FLOOR PLAN — BLENDER GENERATOR
Exact 3D Cutaway Recreation Matching Reference Photograph

INSTRUCTIONS FOR BLENDER:
1. Open Blender (3.6 LTS, 4.0, 4.1, 4.2 or newer recommended).
2. Go to the "Scripting" workspace tab at the top.
3. Click "Open" and select "modern_luxury_villa_generator.py" (or click New and paste its contents).
4. Click "Run Script" (or press Alt + P).
5. The complete 3D scene will be instantly generated with:
   - All rooms (Kitchen, Dining, Sunroom, Stairs, Bathroom, Master Study, Living Room, Patio, Porch)
   - All furniture and accessories (Waterfall marble island, bar stools, dining set, modular sectional sofa, open wardrobe with hanging garments, executive desk, etc.)
   - Full landscaping (Burgundy Japanese Maple tree, perimeter trees, shrubs, lawn, stepping stone path)
   - Professional PBR materials, soft directional sunlight, and interior warm downlights
6. The file is automatically saved as:
   Modern_Luxury_Villa_Sales_Model.blend
   in your Blender project directory.
7. 7 pre-configured cameras are available in the "06_CAMERAS" collection:
   - CAM_01_AERIAL_ISOMETRIC (Active default — exact match of the reference photo!)
   - CAM_02_TOP_FLOOR_PLAN (2D blueprint plan view)
   - CAM_03_LIVING_ROOM (Living room close-up)
   - CAM_04_KITCHEN_ISLAND (Kitchen and marble island)
   - CAM_05_SUNROOM (Sunroom and panoramic garden windows)
   - CAM_06_MASTER_STUDY (Study and wardrobe)
   - CAM_07_FRONT_PORCH (Front veranda and columns)
8. Press F12 to render the active camera to an ultra high-res image.
